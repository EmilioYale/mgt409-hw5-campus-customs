3. What would have been simpler as one agent with tools, and why

  • Ticket 102 for sure. One agent with get_lease, get_cash_balance and request_payment does it in about three tool calls. The team version used three agents, four agent runs, three delegations and 30,007 tokens, and the extra hop is exactly where the duplicate request came from. There's no judgment call in paying rent that needs a specialist.
  • The Boss-as-relay pattern in general. The Boss used 18,626 + 13,562 + 14,858 = 47,046 of the 106,563 tokens (44%) across the run, mostly re-reading replies and re-summarizing them for the next agent. A single agent keeps everything in one context and doesn't need to restate "Inventory says 0 on hand, vendor 1, ETA 9/5…" in every hand-off.
  • Customer Service on these tickets was basically one tool call (draft_customer_message) after re-reading what the others found. That could just be a tool the Boss calls.
  • Where the team still earned its keep: least privilege. Customer Service literally can't see request_payment, Facilities can't make a PO, and only the Boss can resolve_ticket. One agent with all 21 tools is simpler but has a bigger blast radius if a prompt goes wrong. I'd keep the separate roles for money and drop the extra hops for simple tickets like rent.

5. Three new problems it could not solve, and what I'd add

  1. Making October rent. After the run we have $152, and lease 1's next_due is now 2026-10-02 for $2,400. No agent flagged that, because every agent only looks at the ticket in front of it and nothing forecasts cash. I'd add a Treasury/Planning agent that runs on a schedule (not per ticket) with a forecast_cash(days) tool that adds up upcoming rent (leases), open invoices and pending POs against the balance and opens its own warning ticket.
  2. Taking the Yale AI Club's money. The obvious fix for #103 is a deposit from the club to pay for the 12-hoodie restock, but cash only goes out in our model. There's no sales table and no way to record money coming in. I'd add a Sales agent with create_customer_invoice, record_customer_payment (adds to cash_accounts, human-approved like payouts) and record_sale (decrements inventory), so Accounting can see that a 50% deposit on the 10% quote ($522 of $1,044) would cover the $264 PO.
  3. Following through when the tee arrives. PO #1 says ETA 2026-09-05, but nothing moves the shop date forward or receives stock, so Tauhid's tee will never show up in inventory and nobody tells him it's in. I'd add advance_desk_date / receive_purchase_order (PO status ordered → received, inventory +1), plus a Fulfillment role and a send_message tool that only sends after a human approves the draft. Right now "drafts stay on the board" is the rule, so a person has to send it.
